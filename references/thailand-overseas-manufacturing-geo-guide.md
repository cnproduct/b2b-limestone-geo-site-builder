## 1. Global Geopolitical & Trade Background (Why Overseas Manufacturing?)

### 1.1 The US Section 301 Tariff Dilemma for Stone Importers
Since the implementation of US Section 301 tariffs under List 3 and List 4A, Chinese natural stone products (including worked limestone under HS `6802.91.00`, travertine, and slate under HS `6802.99`) imported directly from China face a **25% punitive import tariff**, on top of base Most Favored Nation (MFN) duties (typically 3.7%–4.9%). Furthermore, anti-dumping and countervailing duty (AD/CVD) investigations on engineered quartz and ceramic tiles have created severe compliance anxiety among US general contractors, developers, and national tile distributors.

For high-volume commercial projects (luxury resort hotels, civic plazas, university campuses, multi-family developments), a 25% tariff penalty directly erodes contractor margins or causes project cancellations.

## 2. Legal Origin & Substantial Transformation Standards (19 CFR § 134)

### 2.1 The US Customs "Substantial Transformation" Doctrine
Under US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) regulations (19 CFR § 134) and international rules of origin:
> *"A substantial transformation occurs when an imported article emerges from a manufacturing process with a new name, character, and use distinct from that of the original article."*

Tianya Limestone's Thailand manufacturing facility **does NOT operate as a transshipment or repacking conduit**. The facility conducts deep, physical, value-added industrial manufacturing:
1. **Raw Material Inflow**: Uncut, rough quarry blocks are transported under certified maritime cargo manifests to Laem Chabang Port and delivered to the Rayong stockyard.
2. **Primary Sawing**: Heavy-duty diamond multi-blade gang saws slice raw monolithic blocks into raw dimensional slabs of precise thicknesses (20mm, 30mm, 40mm, 50mm).
3. **Calibrating & Grinding**: Automated 6-head diamond calibration lines grind slabs to strict dimensional tolerances ($\pm 0.5\,\text{mm}$ thickness, $\pm 1\,\text{mm}$ length/width).
4. **Custom Cutting**: 5-axis CNC diamond bridge saws execute architectural sizing, beveling, radius cuts, and drop-face pool coping profiling.
5. **Surface Texturing & Aging**:
   - Heavy vibration water-tumbling drums create authentic rounded edges (Tumbled Finish, P4/R10).
   - Hand-chiseling and antique distressing machines produce undulating pillowed textures (Antique Finish).
   - Automated fine quartz sandblasting followed by diamond rotary brushes produces tactile, non-slip surfaces (Sandblasted & Brushed, P5/R11).
6. **Chemical & Moisture Sealing**: Factory pre-sealing with high-penetration breathable silane/siloxane sealers upon request.
7. **Export Packaging**: Fumigated ISPM-15 compliant hardwood timber crates with heavy foam padding, steel strapping, and waterproof barrier wrap.

### 2.2 Official Certification & Verification Trail
