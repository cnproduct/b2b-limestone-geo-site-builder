## 1. Physical & Mechanical Testing Matrix: ГОСТ ↔ ASTM ↔ EN / DIN

When specifying imported natural limestone for exterior facades, plinths (цоколи), open terraces, steps, and swimming pool surrounds in the Russian Federation, Kazakhstan, and Uzbekistan, project design institutes require cross-verification between **ГОСТ (GOST)**, **ASTM**, and **EN / DIN** standards:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Water Absorption by Weight** (Водопоглощение по массе) | **ГОСТ 30629-2011** (п. 6.2) / **ГОСТ 9479-2011** | **ASTM C97** / **EN 13755** | ≤ 0.5% – 1.5% (для наружной облицовки в морозных зонах) | **< 0.22%** | Prevents capillary moisture ingress, salt efflorescence (высолы), and ice-wedging delamination. |
