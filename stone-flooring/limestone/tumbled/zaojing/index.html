Zaojing is a natural tumbled limestone (Coffer Vault Series · 藻井 (Subtle Caramel Amber & Rich Fossil Bands)) by Tianya Limestone, quarried and fabricated in Shuitou, Fujian, China. Our Zaojing (Coffer Vault Series · 藻井 (Subtle Caramel Amber & Rich Fossil Bands)) limestone pavers and tiles are quarry-direct architectural natural stones crafted for luxury residential estates, commercial hospitality, and high-end landscape projects. The Tumbled finish accentuates subtle warm caramel, golden honey, and rich amber banding, highlighted by large format tumbled pavers with authentic fossil sea-bed character. Dense geological formation provides superior freeze-thaw durability, exceptional dimensional stability, and reliable ASTM C97/C170 compliance (P4 / R10 (Wet Pendulum Certified)).
